## Reference look: `sana-mirza.webp`

Sana's portrait is the reference for the whole set; Amara's (`amara-okafor-lind.webp`) shows the command version of the uniform on the command deck. Every later portrait should match its lighting, rendering and uniform design, with a completely different face.

**Moonbase uniform**
- Dark navy-black jacket with a high standing collar and front zip, worn fully zipped.
- Finely textured fabric, like subtly patterned leather, with thin bronze-gold piping along the seams.
- Epaulettes with gold stripes for rank (two for a department head such as the flight lead).
- A small square metal insignia on the collar.
- A blank dark name tape on the left chest. No lettering anywhere: names are added in the UI if needed.
- Belt with a plain bronze buckle.

**Department markings** (the same uniform, with one badge and patch per department)
- Command: three gold stripes on each epaulette for the deputy commander; a bronze badge above the name tape showing a crater rim with a rising sun (Shackleton's sunlit ridge); round navy sleeve patch with the same crater-and-sunrise emblem in silver.
- Flight wing: gold pilot's wings above the name tape; round navy sleeve patch with a silver wing over a crescent moon.
- Others to be set as each portrait is made, for example engineering, medical and archive.

**To correct later: the sleeve patch.** Sana and Amara currently wear different sleeve patches. The intent is one shared Moonbase mission patch on every crew sleeve, with the department shown only by the chest badge. Pick the shared design, then edit both portraits to match.

**Not Moonbase uniform:** Sefa and Orun are Martian. They wear their own dark robes (plain for Deep Watch, bronze-trimmed for the Assembly), not this uniform.

**Lighting and rendering**
- Hyper-detailed cinematic realism: natural skin texture, sharp focus on the eyes, shallow depth of field.
- Soft key light on the face, cool blue light from windows and panel strips, a warm bronze rim light.
- Near-black navy shadows with cyan and amber accents, matching the game's palette.
- Background softly out of focus, belonging to the character's place: the hangar for Sana, the archive for Callum, the lava-tube arrays for Sefa.

**Earth in the background** is always in impact winter: swirling grey-white cloud and brownish dust, a faint dull blue at the edges and a thin atmospheric rim. Never green land or bright blue oceans.

## How the reference was made

Generated in ChatGPT, one character per conversation, from this prompt, then edited once to change Earth and keep the jacket zipped:

> Hyperrealistic cinematic close-up portrait of Sana Mirza, a 26-year-old South Asian woman, fighter pilot and flight lead of a lunar base in the year 2200 … dark navy-black military flight jacket with a high standing collar and a front zip … bronze-gold piping … epaulettes … gold pilot's wings badge … blank dark name tape … round embroidered flight wing patch showing a stylised silver wing over a crescent moon … No text, lettering, names or numbers anywhere in the image.

For the rest of the cast: attach `sana-mirza.webp` as a style reference, and add *"same lighting, rendering style and uniform design as the attached image, but a completely different person"* plus that character's own face, build, age and setting.
